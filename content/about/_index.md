## Our mission

To end Romania's street-dog crisis by uniting the people already fighting it. We don't run shelters or rehome dogs ourselves — we fund, connect and amplify a network of rescuers, fosterers and partner organisations, and we channel donations and remote adoptions to where they do the most good. We measure success not by how many dogs we save this year, but by the day fewer dogs are born onto the street at all.

## How we started

Strays of Romania began with a simple observation: all across the country, small rescues, lone fosterers and underfunded shelters were doing heroic work — mostly alone, mostly broke, and mostly invisible. What one person could do was limited. What they could do connected, funded and seen was another matter entirely.

So we built the connective tissue: a way to pool donations, list dogs for remote adoption, share sterilisation funding, and give every rescuer in the network a bigger platform than they could ever have alone.
