groups:
  - heading: "Remote adoption"
    items:
      - q: "What is remote adoption, exactly?"
        a: "It's a small recurring gift dedicated to one specific dog. The dog stays with the shelter or foster carer looking after them in Romania; your monthly gift funds their food, care and vet treatment. In return you get regular updates and photos — the bond, without the dog physically moving in with you."
      - q: "So the dog doesn't come to live with me?"
        a: "Correct. Remote adoption is a way to support a dog from anywhere in the world. The physical care stays with the rescuer in our network. If you'd like to adopt a dog for real, see the question below."
      - q: "How much is it, and can I cancel?"
        a: "From €10 a month, though many dogs suggest €15. It's a normal subscription — you can pause or cancel anytime from your account with our donation platform."
      - q: "What do I get?"
        a: "Regular photos and news about your dog, and the knowledge that your gift is keeping one specific animal safe and cared for. Because each subscription is tagged to a dog, we can tell you exactly how they're doing."
      - q: "Can I really adopt a dog to bring home?"
        a: "Yes — but through our partner organisations, not directly from us. Each partner runs its own rehoming process, including any home check and transport. Get in touch and we'll connect you with the right partner for the dog you're interested in."
  - heading: "The network"
    items:
      - q: "If you don't run shelters, what do you do?"
        a: "We're the hub. We raise and pool funds, list dogs for remote adoption, coordinate sterilisation campaigns, and give independent rescuers and small NGOs a bigger platform than they'd have alone. The hands-on rescue, fostering and rehoming is done by our partners."
      - q: "Who are your partners, and how are they vetted?"
        a: "They're registered Romanian rescue NGOs, shelters and trusted independent fosterers. We check their welfare standards and how they use funds before they join, and we stay in contact. (List your real partners and vetting criteria here.)"
      - q: "I run a rescue — can we join?"
        a: "Very possibly. If you rescue, foster or shelter dogs in Romania to a good welfare standard, joining gives you shared funding, remote-adoption listings and a bigger reach. See the 'Partner' option on our Join the Network page."
  - heading: "Donations & the organisation"
    items:
      - q: "Where does my money go?"
        a: "Remote adoptions go to the carer of the dog you chose. General donations fund sterilisation and the shared work of the network. The large majority of all funds reaches the front line — see our transparency page for the breakdown and our registration details."
      - q: "Why sterilisation instead of just rescuing more dogs?"
        a: "Rescue saves the dog in front of you; sterilisation stops the next hundred from being born onto the street. The network does both, but sterilisation is the only thing that ends the crisis."
      - q: "Are you against euthanasia of strays?"
        a: "Yes. Culling is temporary, costly and cruel, and the population rebuilds once it stops. We and our partners only support humane, evidence-based methods."
      - q: "Can I get a tax receipt?"
        a: "Depending on where you live, yes — ask us and we'll explain the options and any partner charities that can help."
