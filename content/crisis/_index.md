origin_eyebrow: "Where it came from"
origin_title: "A crisis built by a bulldozer, not a dog."
origin_p1: "In the 1980s, Nicolae Ceaușescu's <strong>“systematisation”</strong> programme demolished whole neighbourhoods — including much of historic central Bucharest — and forced families into standardised concrete apartment blocks."
origin_p2: "The new flats didn't allow pets. Families who had kept dogs in houses with yards left them behind on the street, un-neutered and unowned. Those dogs bred, and their descendants bred, and forty years later the country is still living with the result."
origin_p3: "It's a crucial point, because it tells you where the solution is: not in punishing the dogs, but in finally breaking the cycle of uncontrolled breeding."
origin_callout_label: "The maths of doing nothing"
origin_callout_body: "One unspayed female dog and her surviving offspring can, in a favourable environment, lead to <strong>thousands of dogs within a few years</strong>. That is why sterilisation — not removal — is the only lever that changes the total."

causes_label: "Why it persists"
causes:
  - kicker: "Cause 01"
    title: "Homes without dogs"
    body: "The 1980s demolitions moved families from houses with yards into apartment blocks that banned pets. Un-neutered dogs were abandoned on the street and began to breed."
  - kicker: "Cause 02"
    title: "No sterilisation culture"
    body: "For decades, spaying and neutering were rare. A single unspayed female and her offspring can lead to thousands of dogs in a few years."
  - kicker: "Cause 03"
    title: "Poverty & access"
    body: "As one of the EU's lower-income countries, many owners can't easily reach or afford veterinary care, so litters go unplanned and dogs go unregistered."
  - kicker: "Cause 04"
    title: "Stop-start policy"
    body: "Laws have swung between euthanasia and release for twenty years. Each reversal reset progress; without sustained sterilisation the population simply rebuilds."

cull_eyebrow: "Cull vs. cure"
cull_title: "Why killing dogs never emptied the streets."
cull_p1: "Culling removes the dogs you can see today. It does nothing about the breeding that replaces them — so within a few seasons the population rebuilds, and the cull begins again. It is expensive, cruel, and it doesn't work."
cull_p2: "<strong>Catch-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return (CNR/CNVR)</strong> is the humane alternative endorsed by welfare organisations worldwide. Dogs are trapped, sterilised, vaccinated against rabies, ear-tipped to show they've been treated, and returned. A sterilised, vaccinated dog can't reproduce and won't spread disease — and over time the population falls on its own."

cull_good_label: "✓ Sterilise & return"
