Culls make headlines. Sterilisation makes the difference. This spring our mobile clinic spent three weeks in and around Constanța, and the numbers tell the story.

## By the numbers

- **412** dogs sterilised
- **412** dogs vaccinated against rabies
- **0** dogs that will ever produce an unwanted litter

Each of those dogs was caught gently, treated by a qualified vet, ear-tipped so everyone can see they've been done, and returned to the place they know. No dog was harmed, and the local population's growth curve just bent downward.

## Why it works

A sterilised, vaccinated dog can't breed and won't spread rabies. Do enough of them in one area — the evidence points to sterilising the large majority of a local population — and the numbers fall on their own, and stay down. It's slower than a cull, and unlike a cull, it actually works.
